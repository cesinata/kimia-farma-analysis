    What changed from the 2023 Data Studio report
    • Source dates are day/month/year. 30/01/22 is 30 January 2022. The old report read it as 22 January 2030, which is why every bar sat on the 22nd and the axis reached 2031.
    • The invoice query used HAVING COUNT(*) > 1. That dropped IN6089, IN6190, and IN6245: 83 units and Rp 4.755.500. The old “products sold” figure of 2.154 is the total with those three removed. This page keeps them: 2.237 units, Rp 90.637.700, 28 invoices.
    • Tramadol Capsule 50 mg and Tetracycline Capsule 250 mg are Capsule on the product master and Tablet on the sales lines. “Unit sold” follows the sales file. “Master packaging” groups those two with Capsule.
    • 25 customer ids on these invoices belong to 8 outlet names. Totals follow the outlet name. Merdeka Pharmacy in Bandung is still first, at Rp 30.030.900.
